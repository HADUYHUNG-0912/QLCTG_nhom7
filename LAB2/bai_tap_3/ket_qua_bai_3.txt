5. KET LUAN VA NHAN XET
==============================================================================
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cau 1 - Chon chuoi du lieu theo thang hay theo quy:
  Chon CHUOI THEO THANG. Ly do:
    - Du lieu co 144 quan sat (12 nam x 12 thang), du de xac dinh chu ky mua vu 12 thang
      va uoc luong Trend + Seasonal + Residual deu o do phan giai cao nhat.
    - Chu ky mua vu 12 thang khong chia het cho 4 nen phan ra theo quy (chu ky 4)
      se lam mat phan thong tin mua vu (chi con 4 diem mot vong).
    - Neu tach theo quy, bien do mua vu van tang dan theo nam, khong giai quyet het
      van de khac phuc cua phan ra.

Cau 2 - STL hay classical decomposition:
  Dung STL (Seasonal-Trend decomposition using LOESS) lam phuong phap chinh,
  ket hop classical decomposition de doi chieu.
  Ly do chon STL:
    - STL giu nguyen phan Seasonal o bien goc dui dang cong, khong ep bien do mua vu
      khong doi (classical decomposition ep nhieu mua vu khac nhau).
    - Xu huong duoc uoc luong boi LOESS: khong ep da bac da thu truong nhan voi tu
      lai khu du lich, phu hop hon moving average co dinh.
    - Bien so 'robust=True' chong lai nhieu du lieu bat thuong, thich hop voi du lieu
      thuc te co dinh bien dong lon.
  Classical decomposition: phuong phap cong bi lech do bien do mua vu tang dan;
  phuong phap nhan cho ket qua tot hon va la luon chon cho du lieu nay.

Cau 3 - Bon thanh phan:
  Da ve 4 thanh phan va luu vao thu muc 'bai_3_figures':
    stl_phan_rava.png      - Observed / Trend / Seasonal / Residual (thang duoc tu)
    stl_phan_rava_log.png  - 4 thanh phan tren thang log
    classical_phan_rava.png - Residual cua cong so voi nhan

Cau 4 - Mo ta:
  a) Xu huong TANG hay GIAM?
     => TANG manh va on dinh. Trend tang tu 122.6 (1949-01) len 500.7 (1960-12),
        gap 378 luat khach, tuong duong ~12.56%/nam. Khong co dau hieu suy giam.
     Luu y: xu huong khong phai la duong thang thuan ma nhoi dan o phia cuoi
     (dot bien nganh khach hang hang khong co du chieu tang). Do la ly do dung STL
     voi ban kinh cua nua doi thay vi moving average co dinh.

  b) Mua vu MANH hay YEU?
     => MANH. F_seasonal = 0.9753 (STL) va 0.9593 (thang log), gan sat 1.
     Dac diem: dinh o T7-T8 (mua he), thap nhat o T11 (mua dong), T1-T2 cung thap.
     Bien do tuyet doi TANG theo nam (46 -> 100 -> 179 luat khach giuaa cac giai doan),
     nen day la mua vu TYC DO (nhieu) chu khong phai mua vu cong. Phai log-transform
     hoac mo hinh nhan de mo ta dung.

  c) Residual co giong white noise khong?
     => KHONG hoan toan. Bang chung:
       - Gia tri mean gan 0 (0.2741) va std nho (6.92), nen phan du da 'sach' phan lon bien dong.
       - NHUNG Ljung-Box bac bo H0 o moi do tre (lag 1: p = 0.0026): con tuong quan.
       - ACF(1) = 0.2483 > 0, cac lag 1-3 deu duong: phan du co xu huong ngay sau
       tinh trang 'nhieu o cap' (over-smoothing) dac trung cua STL.
       - Shapiro-Wilk p = 0.0000 va Jarque-Bera p = 0.0000: phan du KHONG phan phoi chuan
         (nhieu lon nhat nam o 2 dau chuoi - noi Trend chua on dinh).
       - Phuong sai khac nghiet: std phan du tang tu 6.17 len 7.63 giua 2 nua chuoi.
     => Vi vay Residual CHUA phai white noise. Khi uoc luong mo hinh du bao nen dung
        phan du nay lam input vao ARIMA/SARIMA hoac mo hinh bien doi trang thai,
        va nen dung ban phan ra tren thang log de phan du on dinh hon.

  Ket luon chung:
    Chuoi co XU HUONG TANG manh + MUA VU MANH bien dong, phan ra bang STL tren
    thang log cho ket qua tot nhat. Residual con tuong quan va hetero, nen mo hinh
    phai tiep tuc mo hinh hoa phan du.
